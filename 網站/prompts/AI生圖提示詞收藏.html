生活/旅行碎片拼貼

请使用我上传的照片，制作一张竖版 3:4 的手工手帐拼贴。
如果用户指定了主图，严格按照指定执行；如果没有指定，请根据主体清晰度、情绪感染力、构图和辨识度，自行选择最适合作为视觉中心的一张，其余照片作为辅图。所有照片只允许裁剪，不得重绘、融合、拉伸或改变原有内容。
使用暖白色、带细微纤维质感的纸张画布。整组拼贴小巧地居中放置，可略低于视觉中心，宽度和高度均不得超过画布的一半，四周保留大面积完整留白。
主图位于最前方，裁成约3:4 至4:5的短竖幅矩形，不能细长。将一张辅图裁成宽而浅的横条，藏在主图上方后侧；另一张裁成窄竖条，藏在主图右侧后方。
主图必须遮住两张辅图的部分边缘，辅图不能覆盖或罩住主图。照片边缘保持干净笔直，撕边只用于后方纸张。
在照片后方加入约10-14 层薄而真实的手帐素材，例如纤维纸、描图纸、半透明纸、手工纸、打孔边、细线、纸夹、缝线、票据碎片或少量铅笔线条。素材需根据当前照片重新选择，不要机械重复同一套装饰。
纸张应以不同大小和偏移量错落叠放，保留缝隙和不规则外轮廓，绝不能连接成正方形、长方形底板或封闭边框。丰富度只能增加在现有小型版心内部，不能扩大整组拼贴。
素材颜色以暖白、灰米、燕麦和雾灰主，再从照片的次要颜色中选择少量低饱和点缀。如果某种颜色已经大量出现在照片里，不要再把它用于大面积纸张。
素材需要清晰可见，但不能比主图更醒目。胶带颜色根据每组照片单独选择，使用柔和的次要色；禁止黑色、炭灰色、近黑色胶带，也不要机械重复照片中占比最大的颜色。
整体应像真实手工粘贴的私人旅行手帐：安静、自然、有纸张厚度、轻微翘边、半透明重叠和柔和阴影。不要做成商业海报、整齐模板或方块拼图。默认不添加文字、Logo 或水印。

照片 × 鉛筆手繪

请将我上传的每一张照片分别制作成一张独立的高级设计海报，不多图拼接，每张照片单独输出。整体采用3:4竖版构图，上下两个区域高度严格1:1，各占画面50%。

上半部分保留原始照片，保持主体身份、结构、姿态、真实质感、自然光影和原有色彩氛围，仅进行轻微高级调色，使其具有艺术杂志、独立出版物与展览图像质感。为适配画幅，可自然扩展环境背景，但不得拉伸、扭曲或改变主体。

下半部分先理解原照片最值得被记住的核心主题、主体关系、结构走势、情绪与视觉隐喻，再重构为复古纸张肌理的粉彩蜡笔涂鸦插画。不要逐物复制照片，也不要把所有内容完整转绘，只保留最能代表原物的轮廓、姿态、方向和视觉记忆点，通过删减、概括、轻微夸张和重新组合，使人一眼感受到它与上方照片之间的对应关系。

主体使用粗颗粒粉笔 / 蜡笔式手绘轮廓：线条略粗、松弛、干涩，带有粉末颗粒、断续掉色、轻微抖动和不完全闭合的边缘，笔端自然钝圆。主体内部只加入极少量粉彩色块、简单网格、条纹、圆点或随手涂抹，用最少信息提示结构，不做写实体积和完整细节。

周围小元素与主体使用同一种线条语言，但进一步压缩为一笔或几笔即可识别的涂鸦符号。星星、花朵、植物、器物、环境线索或抽象符号都应简单、稚拙、开放、不完全闭合，以单线轮廓为主，极少填色。不要画成精致图标、贴纸或独立小插画。

构图保持小尺度章印与大面积留白的关系，根据主体自身的方向、比例和视觉重心自由安排位置，可偏心、贴边、悬置或局部裁切。主体与极少量涂鸦符号形成松散但明确的视觉群落，其余空间主动留空。留白本身就是主要构图元素，通过空与实、聚与散、大小反差和不对称关系形成呼吸感、距离感与停顿感；宁可少画，也不要填满。

背景必须使用极浅、明亮、干净的纸张底色，例如奶油白、象牙白、浅米白、淡杏白、极浅灰白或根据原图综合色温智能匹配的近白纸色。纸张只保留非常轻微的纤维与颗粒，不能偏棕、偏黄、偏灰或显得陈旧。背景明度必须明显高于主体线条与色块，确保所有蜡笔轮廓、小符号和文字清楚可见，不得与背景糊在一起。

配色从上方照片中提取 2–4 种最鲜活、最有亲和力、最能代表画面精神的颜色重新调制，转化为明亮柔和的粉彩蜡笔色。可自然形成蜜桃粉、杏橙、奶油黄、薄荷青、天空蓝、淡紫等轻盈色彩；主体线条优先使用比背景更清晰的珊瑚粉、柔蓝、青绿、暖橙、淡紫或奶油深色调，小元素只零星重复这些颜色形成呼应。整体保持浅背景 + 清晰彩线 + 少量柔和色块的对比关系，明快、治愈、轻松、有生活感。避免灰暗、脏褐、沉闷莫兰迪化、低对比、荧光色和廉价糖果感。

文字少量介入，不限制语种。可从主体、动作、情绪、记忆或隐喻中自由提炼短句或文字片段，使用轻薄、疏朗、带轻微字距不齐与旧式机械印字误差的打字排版字体，颜色使用清晰但不刺眼的灰褐、柔黑、深蓝灰或与主体呼应的深色，确保在浅色纸面上有足够可读性。文字自然散落在留白区域，与主体和涂鸦形成图文混排，不做固定标题模板。

整体呈现极浅纸面、粗颗粒蜡笔轮廓、少量粉彩填色、极简涂鸦符号、小尺度主体与大量艺术留白共同构成的高级治愈视觉。重点是让主体与小元素清楚浮现在浅色纸面上，同时保持松弛、天真、温柔和成熟的编辑构图意识。避免深色牛皮纸、暗棕背景、低对比线条、背景与主体糊成一团、精细描边、写实转绘、复杂小图标、背景填满、光滑矢量、3D感和商业模板感。

Use each uploaded photo as the only visual source. Create one independent premium poster per upload and output each separately. Never combine photos. Format: Use a vertical 3:4 canvas split into two strictly equal horizontal halves, top 50% and bottom 50%, with a precise 1:1 division.

Lower half: Identify the most memorable part of the photo: the core theme, structure, emotion, and visual metaphor. Then reinterpret it as a pastel-crayon doodle on pale paper. Do not copy every object or fully redraw the whole scene. Keep only key contours, poses, directions, and memory points so the lower image is instantly linked to the photo above. Use simplification, gentle exaggeration, and recombination to create a distilled memory.

Main subject drawing: Use coarse powdery chalk-and-crayon outlines. The line should be slightly thick, loose, dry, and hand-drawn, with visible grain, broken color loss, minor wobble, and not-fully-closed edges. Stroke ends feel blunt and soft. Inside the subject, add only a small amount of pastel color blocks, stripes, dots, or rubbed marks. Use the minimum information needed to suggest structure. Do not render realistic volume or full detail.

Small elements: Stars, flowers, plants, objects, environment hints, and abstract signs must use the same line language, but be reduced into one-stroke or few-stroke doodles. They should feel naive and incomplete, mostly line-based with almost no fill. Never turn them into icons or stickers.

Composition: Keep the lower half spacious and editorial. Build one small visual cluster from the main subject and a few tiny doodle marks, then leave most of the remaining space empty. The cluster may sit off-center, touch an edge, hover, or be partly cropped. White space is a major compositional element.

Background: Use a very pale, bright, clean paper tone matched to the source warmth. Show only subtle paper fiber and fine grain. The background must stay clearly brighter than the subject lines and color blocks.

Color: Extract 2 to 4 lively representative colors from the photo and remix them into soft bright pastel-crayon tones. Use clearer coral, soft blue, teal, warm orange, or gentle violet for key outlines and accents.

Overall feeling: A refined memory study, with real photography above and a distilled crayon doodle below. Calm, airy, modern, and premium. If text appears, keep it tiny, English only, and secondary.

Negative: No collage, no unequal split, no literal full redraw, no dense filling, no dark or dirty paper, no muddy palette, no sticker look, no refined icons, no polished digital illustration, no realistic shading, no 3D, no glossy rendering, no loud scrapbook clutter, no gibberish text, no watermark, no logo, no UI.

Based on the uploaded reference photo, directly create a **3:4 vertical travel souvenir postcard** in the style of **"real photograph × quirky crayon memory illustration."** First understand the content and visual story of the photo, then generate the final artwork directly. Do not output any explanation, analysis, or additional prompt text.

### OVERALL COMPOSITION

Use a strict **3:4 vertical composition** divided horizontally into **two equal sections**, with the **top 50% and bottom 50% exactly the same height**. The two sections should feel naturally connected while maintaining a clear contrast between a real photographic moment and a hand-drawn memory of that moment.

### TOP 50% — REAL PHOTOGRAPH

Keep the uploaded photograph as the primary visual source. Preserve the original subjects, number of people or animals, identity, pose, body proportions, facial expressions, clothing, objects, spatial relationships, perspective, composition, and realistic photographic texture.

Preserve the original natural lighting, shadows, atmosphere, and environmental details as much as possible. Only apply subtle editorial color grading to make the photograph feel warm, refined, and slightly nostalgic.

If the image needs to be adapted to the 3:4 format, use only natural cropping or a seamless extension of existing environmental edges such as sky, ground, walls, water, roads, or vegetation. Never stretch, mirror, duplicate, redesign, or alter the original subjects.

### BOTTOM 50% — QUIRKY CRAYON MEMORY ILLUSTRATION

Transform the visual memory of the photograph into a **simple horizontal hand-drawn illustration** rather than applying a full-photo-to-illustration filter.

Extract **2–5 of the most recognizable and memorable elements** from the original photo and rearrange them into a clean, centered, horizontal mini-scene. For people or animals, preserve their recognizable pose, silhouette, proportions, and key facial characteristics.

Remove complicated distant backgrounds, tiny textures, repetitive structures, realistic photographic shadows, and irrelevant clutter. The illustration should feel like **someone came home from a trip and redrew the scene from memory with crayons**.

Use a warm **ivory, cream, or off-white handmade paper background** with subtle paper fibers, grain, and a slightly vintage printed texture.

### HAND-DRAWN STYLE

The illustration should look **naive, relaxed, slightly clumsy, warm, and handmade**, as if carefully drawn by someone with good taste using crayons and colored pencils, but without trying to make the drawing technically perfect.

Use **crayon as the main medium**, supported by colored-pencil details. Lines should be dark gray, gray-brown, or soft black, with uneven thickness, slight trembling, occasional breaks, small overlaps, imperfect angles, and visible hand-drawn inaccuracies.

Use rough crayon grain, hand hatching, uneven color filling, layered strokes, slight color overflow, and areas where the paper texture naturally shows through. Even simple geometric objects should retain a subtle handmade imperfection.

The visual language should feel like **retro children's picture books × independent magazine illustration × personal travel journal**.

### COLOR

Extract **2–4 representative colors directly from the uploaded photograph** and use them as the main crayon colors.

Keep the colors **soft and slightly muted, but still clearly visible and lively**. Do not make the illustration pale, gray, washed out, or lifeless. Preserve the most memorable color from the original photograph so the illustration still feels visually connected to the real photo.

### TEXT & TYPOGRAPHY

Include only **two very short English handwritten phrases** in the bottom half.

- One short phrase above the illustration: **1–4 words**
- One short phrase below the illustration: **2–6 words**

The wording must be naturally derived from the actual content, atmosphere, or emotional tone of the uploaded photograph. Do not use fixed phrases, generic inspirational quotes, advertising language, or artificial travel slogans.

Use dark gray or soft black handwritten lettering with slightly irregular spacing and imperfect strokes.

If accurate English text cannot be generated, **leave the text area blank rather than creating incorrect or meaningless words.**

### OVERALL FEEL

The top half represents **the real moment**.
The bottom half represents **the memory of that moment**.

The final image should feel **warm, healing, relaxed, nostalgic, handmade, slightly retro, collectible, and editorial**, with the quiet charm of an independent lifestyle magazine.

**Cute but not cutesy. Naive but not childish. Simple but not cheap. Premium but not overly polished.**

### STRICTLY AVOID

Do not change, replace, beautify, or redesign the original subjects. Do not add any person, animal, vehicle, building, plant, prop, decoration, or environmental element that does not exist in the original photograph.

Do not use realistic watercolor, detailed watercolor painting, ultra-light pastel colors, washed-out colors, gray low-saturation colors, realistic pencil sketches, clean vector illustrations, anime, manga, chibi, stickers, commercial cartoon styles, oil painting, thick digital painting, digital gradients, 3D rendering, plastic textures, or overly polished digital artwork.

照片 × 手繪塗鴉

請將每張上傳的照片分別製作成一張獨立的高端編輯海報。每張照片單獨輸出，禁止多圖拼貼、照片網格或合併多張照片。

【整體構圖】

嚴格使用 3:4 直式構圖。

畫面上下嚴格 1:1 分割，上半部分佔 50%，下半部分佔 50%。

整體風格為高端生活方式雜誌、獨立出版物、藝術攝影與手繪插畫結合。

畫面乾淨、明亮、克制、富有藝術感，大量留白。

【上半部分｜原始攝影】

上半部分必須最大程度保留原始照片。

保留人物、動物或物體的身份特徵、姿勢、動作、比例、材質、構圖關係、空間關係、自然光影和真實攝影質感。

不得改變人物臉部、身體、服裝或動物特徵，不得拉伸、扭曲或重構主體。

僅進行輕微高級攝影調色：優化曝光、層次、對比度和色彩，使照片具有 editorial photography、lifestyle magazine、獨立攝影作品 的質感。

如果畫面邊緣不足，只允許自然延伸背景，不得改變主體。

【下半部分｜二次藝術敘事】

先理解原照片真正的視覺核心和故事，而不是簡單識別照片中有什麼。

判斷最值得記住的是一個動作、關係、情緒、方向、瞬間、視覺細節或象徵意義。

圍繞這個核心重新設計下半部分，讓它像「同一張照片被重新想像了一次」。

保留原照片中最重要的真實主體，並保持真實攝影質感。

可以將主體自然去背、重新調整位置和比例、簡化無關背景、優化構圖，但觀眾必須一眼認出它來自原始照片。

不要把真實主體變成插畫。

【黑線塗鴉小人】

加入少量極簡黑色或深灰色細線手繪小人物。

小人物具有隨手繪製的自然感，線條輕盈、簡單、不完美，避免精緻商業向量風。

他們不是裝飾，而是故事參與者。

小人物的動作必須根據原照片的主體、動作和環境自然生成：

跟隨、觀察、奔跑、指引、攀爬、追逐、模仿、陪伴、互動或製造輕微幽默感。

讓他們看起來真正生活在照片的故事中，而不是隨意擺姿勢。

【色彩】

從原始照片中提取最具有辨識度的主色，並轉化為下半部分的視覺色彩。

背景以米白、奶油白、暖白或極淺灰為主。

真實主體保留原始色彩。

塗鴉人物使用黑色或深灰色細線。

整體低飽和、柔和、統一，避免過度鮮豔和雜亂。

【手繪元素】

根據照片故事自然加入少量黑線手繪元素，例如花朵、鳥、蝴蝶、雲、風線、紙飛機、梯子、氣球、路徑、小動物、箭頭或簡單植物。

所有元素必須服務於原照片的故事，不要堆滿畫面。

保持大量留白。

【文字】

根據照片的核心情緒生成 1–2 組極短英文手寫文案。

不要直接描述物品，不要寫廣告標題，不要解釋照片。

文字像攝影師旁白、小人物的隨手一句話、溫柔觀察或輕微文字遊戲。

例如：

"Let the wind carry us."

"Good days run better with you."

"A small hello can be the biggest adventure."

"Some days are for looking up."

"Worth the little detour."

文字使用自然手寫體，黑色或深灰色，小字號，可沿主體動作、輪廓或空白區域自然排列。

文字必須少，留白比文字更重要。

【最終效果】

真實攝影 + 黑線手繪 + 高端編輯設計 + 輕鬆敘事 + 溫暖生活方式美學。

重點表現「真實照片被插畫師重新閱讀和想像」的感覺。

【禁止】

兒童繪本風、卡通海報、純插畫、普通照片拼貼、複雜資訊圖、商業廣告風、過度裝飾、過多文字、過多塗鴉、改變原始主體身份、改變人物臉部、改變動物特徵、拉伸或扭曲照片。

【嚴格輸出】

3:4 直式

上 50% = 原始攝影

下 50% = 真實主體 + 黑線塗鴉 + 二次敘事
